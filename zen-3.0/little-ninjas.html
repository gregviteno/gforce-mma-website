What happens in class

Little Ninjas introduces kids ages 3–5 to martial arts in a fun, safe, encouraging class. They learn the basics, plus balance, coordination, focus and teamwork, all taught at their age level.

Benefits

  • Confidence and Discipline

    Respect, self-control and perseverance.

  • Focus and Listening

    Following directions in structured classes.

  • Balance and Coordination

    Body awareness and motor skills.

  • Skill-Building Fun

    Games and drills that keep learning fun.
