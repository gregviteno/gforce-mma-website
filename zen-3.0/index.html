Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Ask us at your free class or send a message below.

What age can my child start?
As young as 3. Little Ninjas is for ages 3–5. Kids 6 and up join Kids Jiu-Jitsu or Kids Kickboxing, and middle and high school girls can join Crash Girls Wrestling.
Does my child need any experience?
No. Every program welcomes beginners. Kids start with the basics and progress at their own pace.
Can I watch my child's class?
Yes. We have a parent viewing area and a kids' area for siblings.
Do kids have to compete?
No. Competition training is there for kids who want it. It's always their choice.
Can parents train too?
Yes. Adults can take Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Kickboxing or our 45-minute G-Fit 360. All levels are welcome, including first-timers.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the program and how often you train. Your first class is free, and we'll go over options in person afterward.
What should we wear to the first class?
Need from Neil: gi, rash guard, gloves? Loaners for class one?
Do you offer private lessons or birthday parties?
Yes, both. We also run after-school programs, seminars and corporate workshops. Ask us for details.

Founder & Head Instructor

Sensei Neil Gonzales

  • BJJ Black Belt
  • 2nd-Degree Karate Black Belt
  • 30 years training, teaching since 2001. Owning / operating Martial Arts schools since 2003
  • SafeSport, CPR, First Aid, IBJJF Black Belt and Rules / Referee Course Certified

Sensei Neil Gonzales has three decades of martial arts experience and more than 25 years of teaching. He has built his life around training, competing and coaching students.

Neil started training in 1997 and earned his first-degree black belt in Karate in 2003. In 2007, he earned his second-degree black belt and the title of Sensei (Senior Instructor) under Shihan Daniel Schulmann.

In 2013, Neil began traditional BJJ Gi training and earned his BJJ Black Belt under Roberto “Cyborg” Abreu in 2019.

Neil has competed in Karate, Gi and No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, USA Boxing, Kickboxing and MMA. He has taught since 2001, and he believes martial arts training builds confidence, discipline, respect and perseverance, on and off the mats.

Neil has had a long history of success serving different communities including CT and NY and playing the important role as head instructor in multiple locations.

Today Neil runs G-Force MMA & Fitness in Bethpage with his wife, Dawn, and their two daughters. It's a family-owned school where kids, teens and adults train, challenge themselves, build lasting friendships and reach their goals.

“I am fortunate to have dedicated myself to Martial Arts and Fitness, which has brought me and my family numerous rewards and benefits. This passion has inspired me to embrace it as a lifestyle and share it with others.

The culture we provide is something of utmost importance to me. I have seen multiple approaches work. But at the end of the day, I choose to provide an atmosphere myself and my family can be proud of while helping others.”

Sensei Neil Gonzales

Co-Owner & Instructor

Dawn Rizzi

  • Karate Black Belt
  • BJJ Blue Belt
  • 24 years training, teaching since 2003
  • Kids and Preschool age specialty

Dawn Rizzi started training in 2002 at Tiger Schulmann's MMA, where she earned her Black Belt and actively competed in MMA and grappling competitions.

In 2003 she started teaching preschool martial arts classes and found a passion for it. Twenty-plus years later, she teaches both kids' and adult classes.

Dawn uses encouragement and structure to help young students build focus, discipline and respect, and she loves watching a child grow in confidence and discover what they're capable of.

Instructor & Wrestling Coach

Haley Gonzales

  • 2024 USA Wrestling 16U Fargo National Champion
  • 2025 Newsday Wrestler of the Year
  • 2× Blatnick NYS Championship Champion (2024 & 2026)
  • 2× Nassau County Champion (2025 & 2026)
  • 2× New York State Champion (2025 & 2026)
  • BJJ Blue Belt
  • SafeSport Certified
  • Active Women's Collegiate Wrestler

Haley Gonzales started training Martial Arts including Striking and BJJ at five years old with her family. She has actively competed in many Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu tournaments.

Taking on the sport of Wrestling in her freshman year of HS in 2022, her Martial Arts background quickly translated. She quickly burst onto the girls Wrestling scene earning numerous prestigious accolades in her HS career.

A 2024 Fargo National Champion, 2-time Nassau County Champion (2025 & 2026), and 2-time New York State Champion (2025 & 2026). Her hard work and dedication has also earned her a full college wrestling scholarship.

Haley started teaching in 2024 running youth programs and women's only groups with the family. Now on a journey at the collegiate level but when she is home from college, she helps coach wrestling and teaches kids' classes at G-Force. Through G-Force and Crash Girls Wrestling, she hopes to help young athletes, especially girls, believe in themselves and work hard for their goals.
