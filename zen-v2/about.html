Why we opened G-Force

We believe martial arts teaches more than fighting. It builds confidence, helps you overcome challenges and creates friendships, and its lessons carry well beyond the gym.

Our mission is to build strong bodies, develop good character, and bring families together. We want what kids learn on the mats to help them grow into confident, respectful adults, and we want every student to become their best.

We teach martial arts to build confidence, respect and character, in a welcoming, family-friendly school for every age and skill level. Edited from Neil's wording; Neil to OK

The seven virtues we teach

Our values come from Bushido, the traditional Japanese warrior code. They guide how we train, how we treat each other and how we live off the mats.

Gi

Integrity

Do what is right, even when no one is watching.

Yū

Courage

Face challenges with confidence and never be afraid to try.

Jin

Compassion

Show kindness and use your strength to lift people up.

Rei

Respect

Treat instructors, teammates and family with courtesy.

Makoto

Honesty

Be truthful, sincere and accountable for your actions.

Meiyo

Honor

Take pride in your actions and keep your promises.

Chūgi

Loyalty

Stand by your commitments and support your teammates.

Meet the family

Founder & Head Instructor

Sensei Neil Gonzales

  • BJJ black belt under Roberto “Cyborg” Abreu
  • 2nd-degree Karate black belt
  • 29 years training, teaching since 2001
  • SafeSport, CPR, First Aid, IBJJF Rules

Sensei Neil Gonzales has nearly three decades of martial arts experience and more than 25 years of teaching. He has built his life around training, competing and coaching students.

Neil started training in 1997 and earned his first-degree black belt in Karate in 2003. In 2007, he earned his second-degree black belt and the title of Sensei (Senior Instructor) under Shihan Daniel Schulmann. In 2003, Neil also became co-owner of a Tiger Schulmann's MMA school in Danbury, Connecticut.

In 2013, Neil started training traditional Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu under Alexandre "Soca" Freitas and earned his brown belt in 2017. In 2019 he earned his Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt under BJJ champion Roberto "Cyborg" Abreu.

Neil has competed in Karate, Gi and No-Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, USA Boxing (Golden Gloves), Kickboxing and MMA. He has taught since 2001, and he believes martial arts training builds confidence, discipline, respect and perseverance, on and off the mats.

Today Neil runs G-Force MMA & Fitness in Bethpage with his wife, Dawn, and their two daughters. It's a family-owned school where kids, teens and adults train, challenge themselves, build lasting friendships and reach their goals.

Co-Owner & Instructor

Dawn Rizzi

  • Karate black belt, BJJ blue belt
  • 24 years training, teaching since 2003
  • Started with preschool classes
  • Virtus certified

Dawn Rizzi started training in 2002 at Tiger Schulmann's MMA, where she earned her black belt and competed in MMA and grappling tournaments.

In 2003 she started teaching preschool martial arts classes and found she loved it. Twenty-plus years later, she teaches both kids' and adult classes.

Working with kids is still her favorite part. Dawn uses encouragement and structure to help young students build focus, discipline and respect, and she loves watching a child grow in confidence and discover what they're capable of.

Instructor & Wrestling Coach

Haley Gonzales

  • 2024 Fargo National Wrestling Champion
  • 2× NY State & 2× Nassau County Champion
  • BJJ blue belt, training since age 5
  • SafeSport certified

Haley Gonzales started training at five years old and competed in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu tournaments as a kid, winning most of them.

In wrestling, she became a 2024 Fargo National Champion, 2-time Nassau County Champion (2025 & 2026), and 2-time New York State Champion (2025 & 2026). She also earned a full college wrestling scholarship.

Haley started teaching in 2024. When she's home from college, she helps coach wrestling and teaches kids' classes at G-Force. Through G-Force and Crash Girls Wrestling, she hopes to help young athletes, especially girls, believe in themselves and work hard for their goals.
