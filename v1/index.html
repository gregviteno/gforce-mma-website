Our values

Seven virtues in every class

Our values come from Bushido, the traditional Japanese warrior code. They guide how we train, how we treat each other and how we live off the mats. Kanji added by us, confirm OK

義Gi

Integrity

Do what is right, even when no one is watching.

勇Yū

Courage

Face challenges with confidence and never be afraid to try.

仁Jin

Compassion

Show kindness and use your strength to lift people up.

礼Rei

Respect

Treat instructors, teammates and family with courtesy.

誠Makoto

Honesty

Be truthful, sincere and accountable for your actions.

名誉Meiyo

Honor

Take pride in your actions and keep your promises.

忠義Chūgi

Loyalty

Stand by your commitments and support your teammates.

Questions

Parents usually ask

Don't see yours? Ask us at your free class, or send a message below.

What age can my child start?
As young as 3. Little Warriors is for ages 3–5. Older kids join Kids Jiu-Jitsu or Kids Kickboxing confirm ages, and middle and high school girls can join Crash Girls Wrestling.
Does my child need any experience?
No. Every program welcomes beginners. Kids start with the basics and progress at their own pace.
Can I watch my child's class?
Yes. We have a parent viewing area and a kids' area for siblings.
Do kids have to compete?
No. Competition training is there for kids who want it. It's always their choice.
Can parents train too?
Yes. Adults can take Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Kickboxing or our 45-minute Express HIIT. All levels are welcome, including first-timers.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the program and how often you train. Your first class is free, and we'll go over options in person afterward.
What should we wear to the first class?
Need from Neil: gi, rash guard, gloves? Loaners for class one?
Do you offer private lessons or birthday parties?
Yes, both. We also run after-school programs, seminars and corporate workshops. Ask us for details.
